IronRotor

NPC navigation and a dynamic building destruction system in Unity, built to hold 60 fps through heavy simultaneous demolitions.

Shared with permission from IronRotor.

NPC navigation

Each active agent runs its own pathfinding and raycast-based obstacle avoidance on a staggered interval, coordinated by a universal manager. Staggering the updates keeps the per-frame cost flat as the number of agents grows, rather than spiking whenever many agents replan at once.

Or watch on YouTube.

Building destruction

Buildings break apart using pre-fractured meshes, with object pooling to avoid allocation spikes and LOD blending to keep distant debris cheap. The goal was for several buildings coming down at once to cost about what one does.

Or watch on YouTube.
